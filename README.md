# Everyday Wonder, Two Lines

An entry for the make.fun competition
`u-suga-everyday-wonder-two-lines-32f9cbfb` (Everyday Wonder, Two Lines).

Two lines on a small everyday wonder: an elevator stalls between
floors, and the stranger inside is humming my grandmother's lullaby.
Warm, specific, and left unresolved on purpose — the wonder is that
neither of us needed to say a word.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, exactly two
lines, under the 400-character gate) and prints them.
